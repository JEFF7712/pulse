What is Pulse

Every day, your location, emails, calendar, spending, browsing, and health data are collected by companies who use them to sell you things. You never see the full picture they build.

Pulse is a self-hosted agent that connects to your data sources, runs quietly in the background, and surfaces the patterns and insights that actually matter to your life.

It tells you things like why you keep ordering takeout on Tuesdays, or that you're more productive after morning workouts, or that a recurring charge just doubled. No dashboards to check. No apps to open. It comes to you.

Everything the agent knows is stored as readable notes in an Obsidian vault you fully control. You can see, edit, and correct anything it remembers. Nothing leaves your machine unless you want it to.

Your data. Your infrastructure. Your intelligence.
